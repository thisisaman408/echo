ECHO — Autonomous Meeting Workflow Agent


----------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

ECHO is a multi-agent AI autopilot that joins your meetings via
Recall.ai, then autonomously updates your CRM, creates Linear
tasks, posts Slack summaries, and drafts follow-up emails — all
before you leave the call. Every action is auditable: click any
executed task to see the exact audio moment that triggered it.


----------------------------------------------------------------
LONG DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

ECHO is an autonomous multi-agent meeting workflow agent built
for enterprise teams. The problem it solves: knowledge workers
waste 5+ hours per week on post-meeting admin — updating CRMs,
creating tasks, drafting follow-up emails, posting team
summaries. ECHO compresses that to zero.

HOW IT WORKS

A Recall.ai bot auto-joins any Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft
Teams call via URL — no app install, no calendar permission
required. After the meeting ends, five specialist AI agents fire
in sequence:

1. Action Extractor (Featherless / Llama 3.1)
   Extracts every commitment, deadline, and task from the
   diarized transcript using a domain-specialized open-source
   model running on Featherless serverless inference.

2. Stakeholder Classifier (Featherless / Llama 3.1)
   Identifies who said what, their role, company, and who owns
   each action item. Depends entirely on Speechmatics speaker
   diarization to know which speaker is which.

3. Decision Maker (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
   Synthesizes a full cross-system workflow: what to update in
   HubSpot, what to create in Linear, what to post in Slack,
   and what to draft in Gmail — based on all prior agent output.

4. Comms Drafter (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
   Writes the actual follow-up emails and Slack summaries in
   natural language, ready for human review or auto-send.

5. Executor
   Fires real API calls to HubSpot, Linear, Slack, and Gmail.
   Nothing is mocked. Every integration is live and auditable.

THE ORIGINALITY MOAT: AUDITABLE AGENTIC MEMORY

Every executed action is fully auditable. Click "Why?" on any
action in the dashboard — ECHO shows:
  • The exact 30-second transcript snippet that triggered it
  • The full agent debate chain (all 5 agents' reasoning)
  • Audio playback starting at the precise meeting timestamp

This is the trust layer that lets enterprises actually deploy AI
agents into real business systems. No other meeting tool does
this. Otter, Fireflies, and Fellow summarize. ECHO executes —
and lets you audit every decision back to the moment it was made.

SEMANTIC SEARCH ACROSS ALL MEETINGS

Every transcript segment is embedded with Google's
gemini-embedding-001 (3072 dimensions) and stored in pgvector
on Vultr Postgres with an HNSW index. Users search their entire
meeting history in natural language — "What did Sarah say about
pricing?" — and Gemini synthesizes a direct answer from the
top matching segments in real time.

SPONSOR STACK — ALL DO REAL, NON-DECORATIVE WORK

VULTR
Tokyo VM (ap-northeast-1) hosts the entire application stack:
Next.js 16, Postgres 16 + pgvector, Caddy reverse proxy with
automatic TLS for echo.resyl.app. Vultr Object Storage archives
every meeting's audio file for the audit drill-down player.
Remove Vultr and the product does not exist — it is the literal
system of record for planning, coordination, and execution.

SPEECHMATICS
Batch transcribes and diarizes every meeting audio recording
via the Speechmatics API. Speaker diarization (S1, S2 labels)
is what makes the Stakeholder Classifier possible — without it,
agents cannot assign actions to specific people. Speechmatics
is the foundation the entire pipeline runs on.

FEATHERLESS
Serves Llama 3.1 8B Instruct via serverless OpenAI-compatible
inference for the two domain-specialized agents. Async-first
architecture: both agents run in the background after the
meeting. No GPU management. MIT licensed. Fully open-source
and reproducible.

GEMINI
Gemini 2.5 Flash powers Decision Maker (multi-system reasoning)
and Comms Drafter (natural language writing). gemini-embedding-
001 embeds all transcript segments into pgvector for semantic
search. Gemini also synthesizes the natural-language answer
shown above search results. Multimodal-ready for screen shares
and deck PDFs in a future version.

🔄 Agentic Workflows
ECHO's 5-agent pipeline plans its own steps, calls external APIs
(HubSpot, Linear, Slack, Gmail), and manages multi-step tasks
automatically after every meeting — no human intervention.

🌍 Enterprise Utility
Solves the real-world friction point every manager at AI Week
faces daily: 5 hours/week of post-meeting admin. CRM updates,
task creation, follow-up emails — all done autonomously.

🤝 Collaborative Systems
Five specialized agents (Action Extractor, Stakeholder Classifier,
Decision Maker, Comms Drafter, Executor) coordinate through a
shared message bus. No single LLM could do what the pipeline does
together.

🧠 Intelligent Reasoning
Decision Maker (Gemini 2.5 Flash) synthesizes cross-system
action plans from agent outputs, handles ambiguity, and routes
to the right integrations without human intervention.

🧩 Multimodal Intelligence
Speechmatics processes meeting audio with speaker diarization.
Every transcript segment is embedded with Gemini into pgvector.
Audio is archived on Vultr and played back at exact timestamps
in the audit drill-down.
