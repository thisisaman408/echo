================================================
ACT 1 — THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:30)
================================================

[FACE TO CAMERA]

"Hi, I'm Aman Kumar, and I built ECHO for the AI Agent Olympics Hackathon.

Here's the problem: every manager leaves a meeting with good intentions,
no time, and a CRM that hasn't been updated in a week.

ECHO is an autonomous multi-agent meeting workflow agent.
The pitch is one sentence: by the time you leave the meeting, the work is done.

Let me show you live."

================================================
ACT 2 — SHOW THE DASHBOARD + SEND THE BOT (0:30 – 1:00)
================================================

[SWITCH TO SCREEN — echo.resyl.app/dashboard]

"This is ECHO's dashboard. It's deployed on a Vultr Tokyo VM —
Vultr is the backbone of our entire infrastructure: the app server,
Postgres with pgvector, and object storage for audio archives.

I'm going to create a real Google Meet right now."

[Open meet.new in a new tab — copy the URL]

"I'll paste that URL here and hit Send bot."

[Paste URL into ECHO dashboard, click Send bot]

"A Recall.ai bot is now joining that meeting. No app install,
no calendar setup — just a URL. It's in the call."

================================================
ACT 3 — THE LIVE MEETING (1:00 – 2:10)
================================================

[SWITCH TO GOOGLE MEET — friend is already there]

"I'm in the meeting with my colleague. This is completely real.
ECHO's bot is recording right now."

[Talk naturally for about 60 seconds. Say something like:]

"Hey, quick sync. So Sarah from Acme Corp confirmed she wants to move forward
with the enterprise plan. I need to send her the pricing proposal by Friday.
Also, we need to create a Linear ticket for the API integration work —
let's assign that to the engineering team. And make sure we post
a summary to the team Slack channel. Alright, that's it, let's wrap up."

[End the call]

"Call ended. The recording is uploading. The pipeline starts right now."

================================================
ACT 4 — WATCH THE PIPELINE FIRE (2:10 – 3:10)
================================================

[SWITCH TO echo.resyl.app/dashboard]

"Watch this meeting go from scheduled to processing to complete."

[Click into the meeting once agents start firing]

"Five specialist agents just ran in sequence. Let me walk you through each one.

First — the transcript. This was produced by Speechmatics."

[Scroll to Transcript section]

"Speechmatics batch-transcribed the audio and diarized it —
speaker by speaker. S1, S2 — it knows who said what.
Without this diarization, the Stakeholder Classifier has nobody to assign
actions to. Speechmatics is the foundation everything else runs on.

Now the agents."

[Scroll back up to Agent Feed]

"Action Extractor — powered by Featherless, running Llama 3.1.
It pulled the commitments straight out of the transcript:
'Send Sarah the pricing proposal by Friday.'
Domain-specialized, async, open-source — exactly what Featherless asked for.

Stakeholder Classifier — also on Featherless and Llama 3.1.
It identified who Sarah is, her role, her company, and who owns each action.

Decision Maker — this is Gemini 2.5 Flash.
Featherless handled the extraction. Now Gemini does the reasoning:
given these actions and stakeholders, what should actually happen
across HubSpot, Linear, Slack, and Gmail? That's a harder job.
We use the right model for the right task.

Comms Drafter — also Gemini.
It wrote the actual follow-up email and Slack summary.

Executor — fired the real API calls."

[Scroll to Executed Actions panel]

"Linear issue — created. Slack message — posted. HubSpot note — logged."

================================================
ACT 5 — THE ORIGINALITY MOAT — AUDIT DRILL-DOWN (3:10 – 3:40)
================================================

[Click 'Why?' on the Linear action]

"Here's what makes ECHO different from every other meeting tool.

Every other tool — Otter, Fireflies, Fellow — summarizes.
ECHO executes. And then it lets you audit every single decision.

Click Why on any executed action. You see the exact 30-second
transcript snippet that triggered it, the full agent debate,
and the audio plays at the precise timestamp.

This is the trust layer that lets enterprises actually deploy
agents into real systems. You can trace every action back to
the moment in the meeting that caused it."

================================================
ACT 6 — SEMANTIC SEARCH (3:40 – 4:00)
================================================

[Click Search in the nav — go to /dashboard/search]

"One more Gemini feature. Every transcript segment is embedded
using Gemini embeddings and stored in pgvector on Vultr Postgres.

I type 'pricing proposal' — and it finds the exact moment
from the meeting where that was discussed. Semantic search
across your entire meeting history. Ask anything."

[Type "Sarah" or "pricing proposal" — show results]

================================================
ACT 7 — SHOW THE REAL SYSTEMS (4:00 – 4:30)
================================================

[Switch to Slack — #echo-test channel]

"Here's the Slack message that just fired. Real channel, real message."

[Switch to Linear]

"Here's the Linear issue that was created automatically."

[Switch to Recall.ai webhooks dashboard — ap-northeast-1.recall.ai]

"Here's Recall.ai — you can see the webhook deliveries. Every event

[Switch to Vultr console — my.vultr.com]

"Here's our Vultr Tokyo VM. And in Object Storage —"

[Click into Object Storage, show the echo bucket with .mp4 files]

"Every meeting's audio is archived here on Vultr for the audit trail.
The signed URL in the drill-down player comes straight from this bucket."

[Switch to Featherless dashboard briefly]

"Featherless — our serverless inference layer for the two Llama agents."

[Switch to Google AI Studio briefly]

"And Gemini — handling our reasoning and drafting layer."
